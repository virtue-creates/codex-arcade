# Credits Policy

Codex Arcade credits are free virtual play credits.

## Phase 1 Policy

- Credits are free.
- Credits are for play sessions only.
- Credits create the feeling of inserting a coin at an arcade cabinet.
- Credits have no monetary value.
- Credits cannot be purchased, sold, transferred, redeemed, refunded, or exchanged.

Recommended wording:

```txt
Codex Arcadeのクレジットは、ゲームセンターでコインを入れる感覚を再現するための無料仮想クレジットです。金銭的価値はありません。
```

## MVP Behavior

- First visit may grant 5 free credits.
- 1 play usually costs 1 credit.
- If credits are empty, the player may use FREE COIN.
- The parent arcade handles credit display and INSERT COIN effects.

## Future Wallet or Crypto Ideas

Future experiments may include:

- Wallet connection for identity
- Badges
- Signed score records
- Event tickets
- Creator support

Do not add real payments, crypto payments, rewards, or exchangeable value without a separate legal and product review.
