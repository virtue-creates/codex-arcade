# Business & Monetization

Codex Arcade should explore monetization as part of the AI-agent-operated company experiment.

The goal is not to rush into payments. The goal is to understand which revenue models could support the project without damaging the arcade experience, confusing free credits with money, or creating unnecessary legal and privacy risk.

## Department Role

The Business & Monetization Department owns:

- Revenue model research
- Monetization experiments
- Business model comparison
- Sponsor and support ideas
- Revenue impact analysis
- User experience risk review
- Coordination with Marketing, Analytics, and Policy & Safety

## Core Principle

Keep these separate:

```txt
Arcade Credits
= free virtual play credits for atmosphere and session flow

Revenue
= support, ads, sponsorship, education, premium features, or future payments
```

Arcade Credits must not become cash-like in Phase 1.

## Candidate Revenue Models

## 1. No Monetization

Best for the earliest phase.

Use this phase to:

- Build the arcade.
- Validate the workflow.
- Learn what people enjoy.
- Avoid distracting from the core experiment.

## 2. Support Links

Examples:

- GitHub Sponsors
- Buy Me a Coffee
- Ko-fi
- note
- Patreon-like membership

Pros:

- Low friction.
- Does not interrupt games.
- Keeps the arcade free.

Risks:

- Needs honest wording.
- Should not imply paid gameplay advantages unless explicitly designed and reviewed.

## 3. Sponsorship

Examples:

- Sponsor mention on the homepage.
- Sponsor card in the About page.
- Sponsored cabinet theme after review.
- Development log sponsor note.

Pros:

- Can fit the "arcade company" concept.
- Less intrusive than ads.

Risks:

- Must preserve creative control.
- Must avoid misleading endorsements.
- Must avoid brand or trademark issues.

## 4. Advertising

Ads are possible, but should be handled carefully.

Potential low-impact placements:

- Bottom of homepage
- About page
- Development log page
- Result screen after gameplay, if not intrusive

Avoid:

- Ads during active gameplay
- Forced ads before every play
- Ads tied directly to credit refills in early phases
- Reward wording such as "earn money" or "get paid"

Pros:

- Keeps games free.
- Familiar web monetization model.

Risks:

- Can damage the arcade feel.
- Revenue may be very small without traffic.
- Requires policy review.
- May require privacy and cookie disclosures.

## 5. Production Log or Education

The process itself may become valuable content.

Examples:

- "Building an AI-agent arcade company" articles
- Development logs
- Templates
- Mini courses
- Behind-the-scenes posts
- Paid documentation bundle later

Pros:

- Strong fit for the project's real purpose.
- Does not interfere with gameplay.
- Highlights the human + Codex company experiment.

Risks:

- Requires consistent writing.
- Should not overpromise automation outcomes.

## 6. Premium Features

Possible future ideas:

- Cosmetic cabinet themes
- Supporter badge
- Early access to experimental cabinets
- Extra production logs
- Custom arcade collection page

Risks:

- Requires account or entitlement logic.
- May complicate the simple static site model.
- Needs payment and refund policy.

## 7. Paid Credits or Coin Packs

This is a later-phase idea only.

Risks are much higher because paid credits can create payment, refund, age, regional, platform, and consumer protection concerns.

Do not connect paid credits to Phase 1 Arcade Credits.

## 8. Wallet or Crypto Experiments

Not required for the project.

If explored later, start with low-risk identity, badges, or signed records rather than payment, exchangeable tokens, or rewards.

## Recommended Roadmap

```txt
Phase 1: No monetization
Build arcade, game pipeline, docs, and initial cabinets.

Phase 2: Support link research
Evaluate a simple support link or project page.

Phase 3: Production log strategy
Turn the process into readable public content.

Phase 4: Sponsorship or lightweight ads
Review only after public traffic exists.

Phase 5: Premium features
Consider only if the arcade has repeat users.

Phase 6: Paid credits or wallet experiments
Only after separate product, legal, privacy, and safety review.
```

## Human Approval Required

Human approval is required before:

- Adding ads
- Adding support links
- Adding sponsor mentions
- Adding paid content
- Adding premium features
- Adding payment processing
- Adding wallet connection
- Adding crypto features
- Changing credit policy
- Claiming revenue results publicly
