# Privacy and Safety

Codex Arcade should stay playful, transparent, and low-risk.

## Credits

Arcade credits are free virtual credits.

They have no monetary value and cannot be:

- Purchased
- Sold
- Transferred
- Redeemed
- Refunded
- Exchanged

## Payments, Wallets, and Crypto

Do not add real payments, wallet connection, crypto payments, tokens, prizes, or exchangeable rewards without a separate review.

Future wallet experiments should start with low-risk identity or badge concepts, not payment or rewards.

## Analytics

Before adding analytics:

- Decide why analytics is needed.
- Document what will be measured.
- Avoid personal information.
- Add privacy notes if the site is public.
- Get human approval.

## External Assets

Before using external assets:

- Confirm the license.
- Prefer original, generated, or self-made assets.
- Avoid protected characters, brands, logos, and trademark-like naming.
- Document asset sources when needed.

## Game Content

Avoid:

- Real-money gambling mechanics
- Cash-like rewards
- Misleading scarcity
- Dark patterns
- Unapproved copyrighted characters
- Unclear payment language

## Human Escalation

Escalate to the human before:

- Public release
- Analytics installation
- Wallet or payment experiments
- External asset use
- Strong horror, violence, or sensitive content direction
- Any feature with legal, privacy, or safety uncertainty
